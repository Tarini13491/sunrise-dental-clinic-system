About Sunrise

Your partners in organised dental care

Sunrise Dental Clinic is a private centre at 42 Galle Road, Colombo 03. We replaced notebooks with a secure staff desk: every visit has a unique appointment number, a confirmed dentist, and a printed bill that lists consultation fee plus treatment cost.

Walk-ins are welcome for emergencies. Planned care is booked by reception so two patients are never given the same chair.
